Highlights

- Prophet, SARIMA and TimesFM were indistinguishable: sMAPE from 4.70% to 4.83%
- On identical test dates, nine more years of history cut error by 1.23 to 1.71 pp
- That gain is about ten times the 0.13 pp spread among the three leading models
- Neither gradient boosting model beat the seasonal naive benchmark
- Claims required bootstrap and Diebold-Mariano agreement, fixed before analysis
